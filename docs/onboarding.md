title: Onboarding — live in 5 days
url: northline.com/docs/onboarding

Most teams go live on Northline in under a week. A typical rollout looks like:

- **Day 1** — workspace setup, invite the core ops team, connect the primary email inbox.
- **Day 2** — import existing open tickets from a spreadsheet (CSV template provided) or a prior tool via one of the built-in migration connectors.
- **Day 3** — configure routing rules and SLA targets per queue; Northline suggests starting rules based on the imported ticket categories.
- **Day 4** — connect additional channels (web form, Slack) and invite the wider team as requesters.
- **Day 5** — go live; the old inbox or tracker is set to read-only and new requests flow through Northline exclusively.

A dedicated onboarding specialist is included free on Business and Enterprise plans for the first 30 days. Team-plan customers get access to guided setup docs and email support during onboarding.
