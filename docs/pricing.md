title: Pricing & plans
url: northline.com/pricing
---

Northline is priced per seat, billed monthly or annually (annual saves ~15%).

- **Team — $29/seat/mo.** Unified queue, routing rules, shared status board, email + form channels. Good fit for teams under 20 people.
- **Business — $59/seat/mo.** Everything in Team, plus Nova AI (smart routing, draft replies, anomaly alerts), custom SLAs, and API access.
- **Enterprise — custom pricing.** Everything in Business, plus SSO/SAML, a full audit log, custom data retention, and a dedicated support contact.

Every plan includes a 14-day trial with no credit card required. Seats can be added or removed at any time; billing prorates automatically. There is no setup fee on any plan.

Nonprofit and education discounts (30% off Business) are available on request through sales — they are not self-serve.
