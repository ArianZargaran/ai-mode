Nova is Northline's built-in AI layer for operations teams. It runs on your own ticket history, not a generic model, so its suggestions reflect how your team actually works.

Three capabilities ship today:

- **Smart routing** — learns from past assignments and current team load, and routes new requests to the right queue automatically. Teams typically see unassigned-ticket time drop by half in the first month.
- **Draft replies** — pulls context from linked tickets, past resolutions, and the requester's history so agents start from a real draft instead of a blank box.
- **Anomaly alerts** — flags SLA risk before a ticket breaches, based on queue velocity and historical resolution time, not just a fixed timer.

Nova is available on the Business and Enterprise plans. It does not require separate setup — it activates automatically once a workspace has 30 days of ticket history to learn from. Before that, it runs in a cold-start mode using category defaults.
