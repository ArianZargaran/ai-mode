title: Why teams switch to Northline
url: northline.com/switch
---

Northline is an operations platform built for mid-market teams that have outgrown shared inboxes and spreadsheet trackers. Most customers migrate from one of three setups: a shared email inbox, a spreadsheet-based tracker, or a general-purpose project tool bent into a ticket queue.

Three things define the platform:

- **Unified queue** — email, web forms, and Slack requests all land in a single triage view, instead of being split across tools. Each request keeps its original channel context.
- **Automation that adapts** — routing and SLA rules respond to team load and request type in real time, so nothing sits unassigned overnight waiting for a human to notice.
- **Shared visibility** — stakeholders check a live status board instead of pinging the team channel for updates, which is consistently the top complaint customers report about their old setup.

Northline is not a general-purpose project management tool — it is scoped specifically to request/ticket-style operational work: IT requests, facilities, HR case management, and similar queues.
